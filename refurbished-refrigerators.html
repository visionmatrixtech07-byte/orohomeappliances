What “refurbished” means at ORO

It is not simply a used refrigerator. Each one goes through three steps before you see it.

  1. TestedEvery unit is tested before it is offered for sale.
  2. Quality-checkedIt must pass our quality check, so it looks and works like new.
  3. Delivered with 6 months warrantyYour warranty comes with the purchase.

Types of refrigerator we sell

  • Single door. Direct-cool and compact. Suits couples, small families and bachelors.
  • Double door. More space with a separate freezer. Suits families of three to five.
  • Side-by-side. Large capacity for big families and heavy storage.

What we check before we sell

  • Cooling performance and retention
  • Compressor
  • Thermostat and controls
  • Door seals
  • Shelves, drawers and lighting

How to choose

Size: about 165 to 200 litres suits two or three people. A family of four usually needs a double door of roughly 250 to 350 litres. Side-by-side models start above that. Check the space in your kitchen and the doorway before you buy.
