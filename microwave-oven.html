Safe & Efficient Kitchen Appliance Care

Microwaves and OTG ovens operate using high-voltage components and intense heating elements that require certified safety handling. We restore your kitchen's temperature efficiency safely and quickly.

Common Microwave & Oven Issues We Fix

  • ✓ Food not heating or uneven cooking temperatures
  • ✓ Magnetron failure, high-voltage diode, and capacitor repairs
  • ✓ Unresponsive touchpads, membrane keypads, and display errors
  • ✓ Turntable glass tray not rotating or motor humming
  • ✓ Sparking inside the cavity, burning odors, or tripping power breakers

Certified High-Voltage Safety Standards
