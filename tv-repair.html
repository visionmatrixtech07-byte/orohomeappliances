Repair first, replace only when needed. Voltage fluctuations can damage the power supply and motherboard of a modern TV. Instead of jumping to an expensive replacement, our technicians use chip-level diagnostics to repair the existing components wherever possible.

Common faults we fix

  • No display, blank screen or flickering (backlight faults)
  • Horizontal or vertical lines on the panel
  • No sound, distorted audio or out-of-sync speakers
  • TV will not switch on, stuck on standby or blinking red light
  • Wi-Fi errors and smart interface faults

Questions

My TV has a red light but will not turn on. Can it be repaired?

Often yes. This is commonly a power supply or motherboard fault. The technician inspects the TV and tells you whether it is worth repairing before any work begins.

Do you repair smart TVs?

Yes. We repair LED, OLED, QLED, Android and smart TVs, including Wi-Fi and smart interface faults.

How quickly can a technician come?

Our standard is same-day or next-day service, depending on when you book. Call, WhatsApp or use the booking form.

Is there a visiting or diagnostic charge?

Yes, a nominal diagnostic fee applies when a technician visits to inspect the appliance and find the root cause. If you approve the repair, that fee is adjusted into your final bill.

Is there a warranty on the repair?

Yes. Replaced parts and labour carry a service warranty stated on your invoice, typically 30 to 90 days depending on the component. If the same fault comes back within that period, we fix it again.
