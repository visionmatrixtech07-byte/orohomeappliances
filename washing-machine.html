When a washing machine stops, laundry piles up fast. ORO repairs the mechanical and electronic faults of every common type, at your doorstep in Kolar, Mulbagal, Chintamani, Malur, Bangarpet, Srinivaspura, Vemgal and KGF.

Hard water is hard on washing machines. Water in and around Kolar can be hard, and scaling builds up on heating elements, drums and inlet valves over time. Our technicians do deep descaling, drum cleaning and inlet valve replacement so that calcium buildup does not ruin the machine.

Common faults we fix

  • Drum not spinning, agitating or draining properly
  • Heavy vibration, shaking, loud motor or bearing noise
  • Water leaking from the door, inlet valve or drain pipe
  • Error codes, dead touchpad, PCB or motherboard faults
  • Descaling, drum cleaning and odour removal

Preventive check on every visit

We do not stop at the one fault. We also check hoses, filters, seals and balance settings so the machine runs efficiently after we leave.

Questions

Do you repair both front load and top load machines?

Yes. We repair front load, top load, fully automatic and semi-automatic machines, including inverter models.

My washing machine shakes and makes noise during spin. Can you fix it?

Yes. Heavy vibration and bearing or motor noise are common faults we repair. The technician finds the cause during the visit and gives you the cost before starting.

How quickly can a technician come?

Our standard is same-day or next-day service, depending on when you book. Call, WhatsApp or use the booking form.

Is there a visiting or diagnostic charge?

Yes, a nominal diagnostic fee applies when a technician visits to inspect the appliance and find the root cause. If you approve the repair, that fee is adjusted into your final bill.

Is there a warranty on the repair?

Yes. Replaced parts and labour carry a service warranty stated on your invoice, typically 30 to 90 days depending on the component. If the same fault comes back within that period, we fix it again.
