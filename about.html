About ORO Home Appliances

A local Kolar team that repairs your appliances and sells refurbished ones you can trust.

Our story

ORO was built on one idea: Smart choice. Better living. Local appliance repair and the second hand market can be unclear: unreliable freelancers, unpredictable prices, no accountability. We set out to build a professional, standardised service for homes across Kolar, Mulbagal, Chintamani, Malur, Bangarpet, Srinivaspura, Vemgal and KGF.

Three things we stand for

  • Service. Fast diagnosis and repairs with genuine OEM or high-grade compatible parts.
  • Sales. Refurbished washing machines, refrigerators, TVs and ovens, tested and quality-checked, with a 6-month warranty.
  • Trust. Verified, background-checked technicians who treat your home with respect.

No surprises on the bill

Before any repair begins, the technician explains the fault and gives you the cost. If you go ahead, the visiting fee is adjusted into your final bill.
