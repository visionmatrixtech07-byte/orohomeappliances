About ORO Home Appliances

Redefining Home Services in India

ORO Home Appliances was founded on a singular, uncompromising vision: Smart Choice. Better Living. We recognized that the traditional appliance repair and second-hand market in India was plagued by opacity, unreliable freelance technicians, and unpredictable pricing. We set out to build a professional, standardized platform where homeowners can experience seamless, premium service with complete peace of mind.

Built on 3 Core Pillars

Every decision we make—from how we vet our engineers to how we inspect our pre-owned inventory—is guided by our three core pillars:

⚙️ Service

Futuristic efficiency, rapid diagnostics, and repairs using only 100% genuine OEM spare parts to ensure the longevity of your appliances.

🏷️ Sales

Curated, fully inspected certified pre-owned appliances that offer premium home upgrades without compromising your budget.

🛡️ Trust

Verified, background-checked, and highly skilled technicians who treat your home with complete professionalism and respect.

Our Commitment to Transparency

We believe you should never be surprised by a repair bill. At ORO, our pricing structure is clear and upfront. Before any repair work begins, our technicians provide a detailed, itemized diagnostic estimate. If you decide to proceed, your initial diagnostic fee is adjusted into the final service cost.

Looking to the Future

As modern appliances evolve with inverter technology, IoT smart connectivity, and energy-saving designs, our technical teams continuously upskill to stay ahead of the curve. Whether it's restoring an essential refrigerator or installing a premium home entertainment system, ORO is here to keep your home running smoothly.
