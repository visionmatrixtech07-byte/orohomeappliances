Our 4-Step Certification SOP

Unlike unverified second-hand dealers, every pre-owned appliance at ORO undergoes a strict Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) before it ever reaches your home:

  • 1. Selective Sourcing & Procurement

    We selectively acquire gently used appliances from trusted authorized networks and corporate upgrades. We reject units with structural rust, flood damage, or history of major compressor/motor replacement.

  • 2. Comprehensive Multi-Point Inspection

    Every appliance undergoes a rigorous 50-point technical check. Our engineers test cooling retention, motor torque, spin balance, PCB health, voltage stability, and sensor accuracy under maximum load.

  • 3. Authentic OEM Refurbishment

    Any worn-out components, door seals, belts, or filters are replaced exclusively with 100% genuine Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts. We deep-clean, sanitize, and professionally detail both internal cavity and exterior panels.

  • 4. Quality Sign-Off & Warranty Issuance

    Once an appliance passes final quality assurance, it receives the ORO Certified Seal. Every sale is backed by a comprehensive warranty covering mechanical and electrical performance for complete buyer confidence.
